# Hooks

The hook is the first 1.5–3 s: one spoken line + one moving image + the caption. It must
create a question the viewer *needs* answered, and the picture must already be beautiful.

## How the reference reels open

| Reel | First line (spoken) | First image | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| FedEx / Memphis | "Why do FedEx planes from all over America land in the same city, in the middle of the night?" | FedEx plane banking through clouds at night | **Why-question about a visible oddity** |
| Submarine escape | "To get out of a sunken submarine, you have to let the ocean in." | Submarine resting on the seabed, slow push | **Paradox / counter-intuitive rule** |
| AC-130 | "This cargo plane fires … but its guns can barely move … in the dark" | Grey cargo plane from below against the sky | **Contradiction (harmless thing does dangerous thing)** |

Common pattern: the subject is on screen in frame 0, the line names it, and the line contains
a tension word (*why, but, have to, never, only, same*).

## Formulas (write 5 hooks, each with a different one)

1. **Why-oddity** — "Why do ALL ___ ___?" (FedEx). Name a pattern viewers have seen but never questioned.
2. **Paradox** — "To ___, you have to ___ (the opposite)." (submarine)
3. **Contradiction** — "This ___ can ___, but ___." (AC-130)
4. **Hidden system** — "Every night at 1 AM, 140 planes land in one city." Number + time + place.
5. **You-statement** — "Your ___ is ___ (and you never noticed)." Puts the viewer in the story.
6. **Scale shock** — "There are 84 miles of conveyor belts under this building."
7. **Wrong-belief correction** — "The map you learned in school is lying to you."
8. **Mid-action drop-in** — start in the middle of the most dramatic moment, rewind later.

## Hook checklist

- ≤ 14 words, speakable in ≤ 3 s at 1.1× speed
- the subject is visible in frame 0 (no "imagine…" over black)
- one concrete noun the viewer can picture (plane, submarine, city, package)
- creates exactly ONE open question (not three)
- doubles as the end of the loop sentence: "…which is why + <hook>" must be grammatical
- no clickbait the video can't pay off

## Format per hook (what you show the user)

```
H3 — Paradox
SAY:  To get out of a sunken submarine, you have to let the ocean in.
SHOW: sub on the seabed, camera slowly pushes toward the hatch, silt drifting
TEXT: punch captions, "submarine" + "ocean" in accent
LOOP: "…which is why to get out of a sunken submarine…" ✓
```
