# HOOK
Black boxes aren't black.

# SETUP
They're bright orange, so search teams can spot them in the wreckage.
And they ride in the tail, the part most likely to survive.

# REHOOK
But the orange box is just armor.
The real recorder is this little chip, and it has to pass three tests.

# TEST 1 · COMPARISON
First, a hit of 3,400 g.
A fighter pilot blacks out at 9.

# TEST 2
Then fire. 1,100 degrees, for a full hour.

# TEST 3
Then the ocean, 20,000 feet down.

# STAKES
Underwater, a beacon pings every second, for 90 days.
Then it goes silent.

# LOOP OUTRO
So the one thing it can't survive is not being found. Which is why…
