# HOOK
Every airport on Earth banned you and your giant pocket puh after the skyscraper incident.
So you build a floating steel airport in the middle of the ocean.

# DECK & EMALS
You weld 100,000 tons of steel into a ship 333 meters long, packing 75 fighter jets and 4,500 crew.
Big problem.
Normal jets need two kilometers of runway to take off, and your whole ship is barely a third of a kilometer.
So you bury giant electromagnetic catapults right under the deck.
They blast a loaded fighter jet from zero to 240 kilometers per hour in just two seconds.

# LANDING & AAG
Awesome. Now how do they land without flying straight into the ocean?
Brakes aren't nearly fast enough.
So you stretch three thick steel cables across the deck, connected to massive water turbines below.
When the jet slams down, its tailhook catches a cable and yanks it to a dead stop in 90 meters.
Plot twist.
Pilots actually hit full throttle the second their wheels touch down.
That way, if they miss all three cables, they blast straight back into the sky.

# NUCLEAR REACTORS
One last problem.
Pushing a floating city across the ocean burns insane amounts of fuel.
So you drop two nuclear reactors into the hull.
Now your airport sails nonstop for 25 years without refueling once.
