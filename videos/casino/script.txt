# HOOK
Your friend owes his grandma 100 dollars for his pocket puh.
Instead of getting a job, he bets it at your table.
He wins, and heads for the door.
So you build a place he never leaves.

# CLOCKS AND LAYOUT
He checks the time, so you rip out every clock and window.
Old casinos built confusing mazes to trap players.
Plot twist.
In 1998, the Bellagio spent 1.6 billion doing the opposite.
High ceilings, open paths.
Relaxed players bet way more.

# CHIPS AND DRINKS
Still, losing real cash hurts.
So you swap his 100 dollar bill for plastic chips.
His brain stops feeling the loss.
And the drinks are free.

# ROULETTE HOUSE EDGE
But on a 50 50 bet, he could actually win.
So on roulette, you sneak in a green zero.
2.7 percent house edge.
Vegas added double zero.
5.26 percent.
Some Strip tables even added triple zero.
7.69 percent.
The house always wins.

# SLOTS VIRTUAL REEL
Next, you add slot machines.
Old reels had 22 stops, too easy to hit.
A 1984 patent mapped them to a giant hidden virtual reel.
Now the jackpot stops one notch off.
Near miss.

# LOSS DISGUISED AS A WIN
Bet 1 dollar, win 40 cents, and bells go crazy.
Scientists call it a loss disguised as a win.
His brain celebrates, while his wallet shrinks.

# OXYGEN MYTH
People swear you pump oxygen into the vents.
Total myth.
One spark would blow the roof off.
Instead, you pump cool air and signature scents.

# CARD COUNTING AND SECURITY
Wait.
He's counting cards at blackjack.
Technically, completely legal.
You own the building, so you ban him anyway.
Ceiling domes scan his face, and RFID chips track every bet.

# SCALE AND FINALE
American casinos made 72 billion dollars in 2024.
Your friend goes all in with his pocket puh.
He loses it to his grandma.
Congratulations.
You've built a money trap.
You call it, the casino.
Follow, or I'll put your pocket puh on green zero.
