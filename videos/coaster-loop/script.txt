# HOOK
Roller coaster loops aren't circles.

# SETUP
In 1895, Coney Island built a round one. Riders came off with neck and back injuries.

# REHOOK
Here's why. At the top, you only need just enough speed not to fall. But by the bottom, you've picked up a lot more.

# PHYSICS
In a circle, that always adds up to at least 6 g. Six times your body weight. No matter how big the loop.

# TURN
So engineers bent it into a teardrop. Wide at the bottom, where you're fast. Tight at the top, where you're slow.

# PAYOFF
In 1976, the Revolution in California became the first modern looping coaster, built for under 5 g.

# LOOP OUTRO
So look closely next time.
