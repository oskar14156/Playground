## World
- Set: one persistent dot-matrix Earth (Natural Earth land dots), graticule, ~25 warm city lights,
  fresnel atmosphere, star field; it morphs between globe and Mercator map.
- Recurring objects: gold great-circle arc + plane, pink rhumb line, cyan helpers (Arctic Circle, Tissot circles).
- Palette `night`: navy → purple horizon, gold `#ffc65c`, pink `#ff4f8b`, cyan `#7fe3ff`.

## Loop
- Last line "Which is why…" → hook. Tilt, spin and camera return to the opening values before the
  last 16 frames; LoopBridge dissolves into frame 0.
