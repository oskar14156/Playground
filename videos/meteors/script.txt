# HOOK
Most shooting stars are smaller than a grain of sand.

# SETUP
These ones are dust from a comet called Tempel-Tuttle. Every November, Earth drives straight through its trail.

# SPEED
Each grain hits the air at 70 kilometers per second. About 70 times faster than a rifle bullet.

# REHOOK + MECHANISM
But it doesn't glow from friction. The air in front of it gets squeezed so hard it heats up, past 1,600 degrees. All of that, 100 kilometers above your head.

# ESCALATE
Most nights you'll catch a handful an hour. But in 1966, people saw thousands per minute.

# PAYOFF
That's the Leonids. This year they peak on the night of November 17th.

# LOOP OUTRO
So look up, and remember…
