# HOOK
Grandma survived the Titanic, and moved into a penthouse in the clouds with your giant pocket puh.
So you build an even taller skyscraper right next door to peek in her window.

# FOUNDATION
You stack 163 floors, weighing 450,000 tons, straight onto desert sand.
One problem. There is no solid bedrock down there.
So you drill 192 concrete piles, 50 meters deep, tied to a concrete raft nearly 4 meters thick.
Your entire tower literally hangs from the sand by friction.

# NIGHT ICE CONCRETE
Now it's 50 degrees outside in Dubai, and your concrete cooks before it reaches the top.
So you ban daytime pours.
You mix tons of crushed ice right into the wet concrete, and only pump it at night.

# CONFUSING THE WIND
At 828 meters, steady wind creates giant swirling whirlpools called vortex shedding.
A straight tower would start shaking like a guitar string until it snaps.
So you literally gaslight the wind.
You shape the building like a giant Y, with 27 spiraling setbacks.
Every few floors the shape changes, so the wind gets confused and never finds a rhythm.
Even then, the tip still sways nearly 2 meters in a storm.

# ELEVATORS & WINDOWS
Stairs would take an hour, so you install double deck elevators shooting up at 36 kilometers per hour.
Next, you bolt on 24,000 glass panels.
Washing them takes a crew 3 full months by hand.
The second they clean the bottom window, the top is dirty again, so they start right over.

# PAYOFF
Congratulations.
You've built a half mile tall ice concrete skyscraper, just to spy on grandma.
You call it, the Burj Khalifa.
