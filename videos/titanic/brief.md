# Titanic didn't sink in one piece
- Question: what tore Titanic in half — and why did nobody believe it for 73 years?
- Length: ~40 s · Language: en · Voice: chatterbox default · Music: dark
- Palette: night (cold) · Captions: punch
- Audience: everyone who has seen the movie; knows "iceberg", not the mechanism
- Protagonist: the ship itself, followed from the surface to the seabed
- Loop: "…So the truth sank with her. Which is why…" → "For 73 years, everyone thought Titanic sank in one piece."
- Tone: respectful — 1,500+ people died. No people in danger on screen, no gore; survivors only as small figures in a lifeboat.

## Facts
1. Wreck found 1 Sep 1985 (Ballard / Michel), until then assumed to have sunk intact; 1912 → 1985 = 73 years — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wreck_of_the_Titanic
2. Depth ≈ 3,800 m (12,500 ft) — NOAA / WHOI via the same article
3. Two main pieces ≈ 600 m (1,970 ft) apart, facing opposite directions — same article
4. Designed to stay afloat with up to 4 flooded compartments; the iceberg opened 5 (some say 6) of 16 — https://blogs.staffs.ac.uk/automotive/2016/04/15/titanic-the-unsinkable-ship-and-the-lessons-for-engineers ; https://www.twinkl.com/teaching-wiki/the-titanic-sink
5. Bulkheads were not capped, water spilled over the tops as the bow sank — same sources
6. Break began at a shallow angle, ~10–15° (Hackett & Bedford simulations; forensic studies); the 1997 film shows ~45° — https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/breakup-of-titanic.html ; https://timmaltin.com/2019/05/01/titanic-broke-in-half/
7. Survivors incl. Jack Thayer reported the break; the 1912 inquiries preferred officers/Wilding and concluded she sank intact — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Thayer ; https://www.encyclopedia-titanica.org/community/threads/the-inquiries-finding-that-the-titanic-sank-intact.39129/page-2
8. Exact break location (near the 3rd/4th funnel) is debated → script says only "the hull", visuals break it behind the 3rd funnel without naming it.

## Hooks considered
- H1 Wrong-belief: "For 73 years, everyone thought Titanic sank in one piece." ← picked (loopable, sets up the twist)
- H2 Paradox: "Titanic sank because her stern was out of the water."
- H3 Contradiction: "The movie got Titanic's last minutes wrong — by 30 degrees."
