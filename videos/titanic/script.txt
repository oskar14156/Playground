# HOOK
For 73 years, everyone thought Titanic sank in one piece.

# SETUP
Then in 1985, they found her. 3,800 meters down, in two pieces, 600 meters apart.

# REHOOK
So what tears a steel ship in half?

# CAUSE
The iceberg opened 5 of her 16 compartments. She was built to survive 4.
As the bow sank, water spilled over each wall into the next.

# ESCALATE
The stern rose out of the sea. Thousands of tons of steel, hanging in the air.
The hull bent like a ruler over a table edge, until it snapped.

# COMPARISON
Not at the steep 45 degrees from the movie. Wreck studies say about 15.

# TWIST
