# HOOK
Grandma won your giant pocket puh at the casino, and sailed off to New York.
So you build an unsinkable ship to chase her down.
You start with a 269 meter steel hull, packed with 3 million rivets.

# BULKHEADS
But one tiny leak can sink the whole boat.
So you slice the hull into 16 watertight compartments.
Even if 4 flood, she still floats.
But here's the plot twist.
You don't seal the tops.
The walls stop just 3 meters above the water, like a giant ice cube tray.
Flood 5, and water spills right over the top into the next.

# FOURTH FUNNEL
Your 29 coal boilers only need 3 smokestacks.
So why bolt on a fourth?
Pure aura farming.
The fourth funnel isn't even hooked to the boilers.
It's just a massive air vent, so the ship looks cooler.

# LIFEBOATS
Wait. Why only 20 lifeboats for over 2,200 people?
British law from 1894 only required 16.
You actually beat the legal minimum, and cut the rest, so they wouldn't ruin the view.

# BINOCULARS
You cruise at 22 knots into an ice field.
Your lookouts reach for the binoculars, except the locker is locked.
An officer got reassigned before launch, and pocketed the key.

# TURBINE PARADOX
An iceberg appears 400 meters ahead.
You slam full reverse, and crank the wheel.
Huge mistake.
Your center propeller runs on a steam turbine that can't reverse, so it just stops.
That kills water flow over the rudder, wrecking your steering.

# COLLISION & POOL
Ice rips open 6 compartments.
The ice cube tray spills over, and she sinks 3,800 meters down.
At least you built a heated indoor swimming pool.
Fun fact, it's still full of water.
