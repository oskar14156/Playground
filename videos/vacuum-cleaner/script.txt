# HOOK — the problem
Your friend's giant pocket puh is shedding.
His entire apartment is turning blue.
So you grab a broom.
But the fur just flies into the air.

# FAN — first attempt
So you put a spinning fan in a box and attach a hose.
The fan blows air out, lowering the pressure inside.
Room air rushes in and carries the fluff with it.
But you've built a machine that shoots fur out the back.

# DUST COLLECTOR
So you seal a dust container around the airflow, with a filter to catch the fluff.
Now the blue fur stays inside.
But the filter clogs, and the airflow slows down.

# CYCLONE
So you make the air spin in a round chamber, like a tiny tornado.
The heavier fluff gets thrown toward the walls and drops into the bin.
The smaller dust can keep going.

# FINE FILTER
But your friend doesn't want to breathe that dust.
So you add a fine filter before the air escapes.
Congratulations. You can breathe in your own living room again.

# BRUSH
But the carpet is still blue.
Fur is tangled deep between the carpet fibers.
So you add a rotating brush that shakes it loose.
Now the airflow can carry it away.

# MOTOR SAFETY
But the hose gets blocked.
The air stops flowing properly, and the motor starts overheating.
So you add a thermal cutout that switches it off before it gets too hot.
You clear the blockage.
And then you make the nozzle bigger.

# PAYOFF — obviously cartoon physics
Finally, the floor is spotless.
The sofa is spotless.
Even the giant pocket puh is gone.
You look inside the transparent dust bin.
Two googly eyes stare back at you.
Your friend says, "You cleaned the wrong thing."
