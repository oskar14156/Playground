# HOOK
You finally stole your giant pocket puh back from Grandma's penthouse.
Now she wants revenge, so you build the safest vault on Earth to hide it.

# WALLS
First, you pour a two story bunker using 4,200 cubic yards of concrete, wrapped in solid granite and 750 tons of steel.
The outer walls are over a meter thick, designed to survive bombs, drills, and angry grandmas.

# DOOR & CODES
Next, you bolt on a blast proof steel door weighing 20 tons.
It laughs at blowtorches and diamond saws.
One problem. What if Grandma kidnaps you and forces you to type the code?
So you split the combination between 10 different people.
No single human on Earth knows the whole password, and all 10 have to enter their piece separately.

# UTILITIES & ARMY
Wait. What if she cuts the city power and water to shut down your alarms?
So you build your own emergency power plant and private water supply right inside the bunker.
Still not paranoid enough?
So you place the entire vault right next door to an active army base, surrounded by motion sensors, radar, and federal police.

# GOLD & TWIST
To really flex, you stack 4,500 tons of solid gold bars right next to your pocket puh, worth hundreds of billions of dollars.
Just one tiny flaw.
You forgot who has code number 10, so now even you can't open the door.

# PAYOFF
Congratulations.
You've locked yourself out of the world's ultimate fortress, just to hide your pocket puh.
You call it, Fort Knox.
