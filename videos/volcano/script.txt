# HOOK
The scariest volcanoes have the slowest lava.

# SETUP
In Hawaii, lava pours out at about 1,100 degrees. Some flows creep just 50 meters an hour. You could walk away.

# REHOOK
So why did Mount St. Helens blow itself apart?

# MECHANISM
Every magma carries dissolved gas, like a shaken soda. In runny magma, the bubbles slip out. In sticky magma, they're trapped.

# ESCALATE
The pressure keeps rising. In 1980, the north side of St. Helens slid away, and all that gas burst out at once. The blast flattened forests up to 30 kilometers away.

# COMPARISON
The mountain lost 400 meters of height. More than the Eiffel Tower.
